# Marriage dispensation, Monterrey, 15 January 1693 — a sworn pedigree

**Source:** *Guadalajara. Marriage Dispensation Records 1656–1699*, FamilySearch Full-Text,
ark **`3:1:3Q9M-CSXG-DW7X-R`** (compound item of 1,179 images). Read from FamilySearch's AI transcript,
7 Sep 2026. The transcript is rough on a 1690s hand — names and relationships are clear, connective
wording is not. **The original page has not yet been read by eye.**

**Header:** "…Monterrey, y Enero 15 de 1693. **3.º con 4.º de consanguinidad**…" Presented before
**Don Felipe Galindo, of the Order of Preachers, Bishop of Guadalajara**, during his general visitation.

## The petition, in the petitioner's own voice

> "**Bartolomé de Quintanilla**, vecino de esta ciudad, **hijo legítimo del Capp[itá]n Bartolomé González
> de Quintani[lla] y de Nicolasa Fernández**, ellos mismos vecinos de dicha ciudad… digo que… tengo
> tratado y concertado contraer matrimonio… con **Antonia García de Sepúlveda, doncella, hija legítima de
> Don Diego García de Sepúlveda y de Elena de la Garza**, vecinos en esta jurisdicción; y porque la
> susodicha y yo nos hallamos ligados en **parentesco de sangre de tercero con cuarto grado**,
> necesitamos de apostólica dispensación.
>
> Que procede de dicho parentesco de que el **Capitán Blas de la Garza, mi bisabuelo**, fue hermano
> legítimo de **Francisco de la Garza**, abuelo de la dicha Antonia García de Sepúlveda contrayente.
>
> Y del dicho **Blas de la Garza y de Beatriz González, mis bisabuelos**, procedió de legítimo matrimonio
> **Beatriz González, que fue casada con el Capp[itá]n Gregorio Fernández, mis abuelos**, [de quienes]
> procedió de su legítimo matrimonio **la dicha Nicolasa Fernández, mi madre**.
>
> Y por la otra parte, el dicho **Francisco de la Garza fue casado con Juana García**, abuelos de la
> contrayente, de quien procedió la dicha **Elena de la Garza, madre de la contrayente**."

## Why it matters

**1. A fourth independent attestation of the couple at the top of the proven chain.** The petitioner is
Bartolomé the younger's son (b. 1676), naming his parents as **Capitán Bartolomé González de Quintanilla
and Nicolasa Fernández** — after the 1712 will, the 1725 will, the 1689 will of his uncle Nicolás and the
1710 dowry receipts.

**2. It documents four generations of Don's own descent, sworn in a legal filing.** The petitioner's
mother is **Nicolasa Fernández de Tijerina**, who is also the mother of **Antonio** — Don's direct
ancestor. So the same pedigree is Don's:

> **Capitán Blas de la Garza × Beatriz González**
> → **Beatriz González × Capitán Gregorio Fernández [de Tijerina]**
> → **Nicolasa Fernández de Tijerina × Bartolomé González de Quintanilla**
> → **Antonio** (bap. 1684) → Francisco Miguel → José Antonio → Dámaso → Inocencio → Hector Sr. →
> Manuel → Eleanor → Don

**3. It upgrades a claim the site had graded Probable.** `quintanilla.html` said: "Blas de la Garza
Falcón and Alonso de Treviño married two sisters, Beatriz and Anastacia González-Hidalgo Navarro of
Saltillo. And I descend from both couples: Blas and Beatriz through the Tijerina wife of 1672" — with the
whole passage marked Probable, on compiled descendancies. **This 1693 petition states exactly that
descent as sworn fact.** Blas de la Garza Falcón is a son of Marcos Alonso de la Garza y del Arcón of
Lepe, Huelva — a founding family of Nuevo León.

**4. It corroborates the 1725 will independently.** That will gave Nicolasa's parents as *Gregorio
Fernández de Tijerina and Doña Beatriz González*; this petition, thirty-two years earlier, says the same.

## What it does NOT do

It does **not** touch **Ana de Quintanilla's** parents. The kinship here runs up the **Garza/Tijerina**
side — the bride's family — so the recitation never goes near the Quintanilla mother's line.

⇒ **The method is proven, though.** A dispensation whose impediment ran through the **Quintanilla** side
would have to recite Ana's ancestry to prove the degree. Worth hunting for one among the other children
and grandchildren of Bartolomé and Nicolasa.
